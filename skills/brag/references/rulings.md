# Founder rulings for product videos

These carry into every project (scaffold.sh copies this file). They override anything in a brief. When the founder gives a new ruling during a build, append it here, with the date and his words, in the same turn, before acting on it. Machine-checkable parts also go into rulings.json.

Sources: the owner's notes from the two Claudemy video projects (2026-10-15 and 2026-10-23) and his working rules on iteration.

1. No emdashes and no double hyphens in visible text. Enforced by scripts/copylint.py.
2. Nothing confidential appears: no client names, no material marked confidential (on the Claudemy tour, one confidential course was kept out of every frame). Project-specific words go in rulings.json "forbidden_words".
3. Match the reference's pace, not only its colors. Measure the reference and every render with the same instrument (scripts/pace.py) and compare to the reference, not to the previous attempt. The Sonos reference: about 4.1 s shots, frames moving about 66 percent, small chapter labels, minimal type, photographic objects.
4. Ken Burns drift on stills is not enough. Motion comes from real movement: generated video clips, or UI that animates. (Founder, after render 1: "the images need much more movement".)
5. When a different look is asked for, do not use the product site's look. The look follows the reference.
6. Voices in the video are the product's own real output. Never invent testimonials or dialogue. No narration unless the brief asks for it.
7. Cut speech at transcript word timestamps, never at a loudness dip. End a clip on a natural pause. The music hands off to the voice and back with no dip. (Founder, after renders 2 and 3: the voice stopped mid-word at "shake" because the cut was chosen from loudness.)
8. Smooth tracked screens. Report the frame-to-frame acceleration before and after smoothing. (Render 3: 0.758 px to 0.0018 px, residual mean 0.45 px.)
9. Spend gate: state the cost, test one clip in two ways, let the founder pick, log the spend. Nothing that costs money runs without an approved line in BRIEF.md "Spend approved". See spend-gate.md.
10. Done means is measured. Define it before building, check it after every round, report each result as a number. "It looks right" is not done.
11. Every ruling goes into a file the code reads, in the same turn. Never only into a brief or a prompt. Each brief carries all prior rulings, not just the latest.
12. The same failure twice means stop that worker and diagnose the root cause with a fresh one. At most three rounds on one class of fix, then report what was tried, what failed and the state. Do not start round four.
13. Prove the shape by hand before building machinery.
14. One worker per job. Two workers on overlapping files cost more than they save.
15. Render settings: 1920x1080, 30 fps, h264 crf 17, yuv420p, AAC 192k at 48 kHz, loudness normalised to -16 LUFS with true peak at most -1.5 dBTP, the poster frame baked into frame 0 from a hero frame.
16. Built product: real copy only. Every on-screen claim traces to the product's README, spec or demo, and carries a [src: ...] tag in SCRIPT.md. Concept product: all data on screen is invented, the label "Concept preview" is on every scene, and no prices, customers or numbers about speed or accuracy appear.
17. Report what was not verified. Any failed or skipped check goes into the final summary verbatim.

Rulings from a reference-matching explainer (route R4), added 2026-10-23. Numbers 18 to 26 came from the owner's corrections; numbers 27 to 38 come from the design-chain study and carry their source in parentheses (paths are relative to the kipi-system repository).

18. Match the reference's PRODUCTION, not only its grammar. The measured failure: a video with the right beats, labels and palette moved on 51 percent of frames where the reference moved on 83 percent, because flat static cards stood where the reference had a moving 3D camera. Study how the reference is drawn (STUDY.md, templates/STUDY.md) before designing, and compare with the same instrument every round (scripts/compare.py).
19. Prove one beat by hand before the full build: one beat, rendered to stills and a short clip, looked at, before the other beats exist.
20. Show stills before the full render, and wait for the answer. A full render is not started on silence.
21. No speed or multiplier claim on screen or in the voice ("10x faster", "in minutes") unless the user supplies a source for it. With a source, it carries a [src: ...] tag and goes in rulings.json "allowed_phrases" as the exact phrase, so a bare variant still fails.
22. Sound defaults to the voice plus a quiet bed. No whooshes, clicks or other foley unless the user asks for them.
23. Deliver to ONE named folder, verify the file is there with ls (name and size), and keep a project-local copy. Say the folder in the report.
24. Guard disk space. A render writes gigabytes; check free space before it and during it (scripts/render_pipe.py does, and stops cleanly under a floor), and prefer streaming to ffmpeg over thousands of PNGs when space is tight.
25. The verifier passing is necessary, not proof the look is right: three videos passed verify.py and were rejected by eye. A run with no FAIL means the files are in order and the checks held; the video still gets looked at, by the person it is for, before it is called done.
26. Record each correction as a numbered ruling in this file in the same turn it is given, with the date and the person's words, before acting on it.
27. Quote or time-stamp the reference; do not paraphrase it. A technique in STUDY.md names the time it happens, and a brief line that rests on the reference carries its quote. (design-chain brief step: q-system/.q-system/scripts/design-chain-gate.py chain_problems, "brief.md does not quote verbatim"; plugins/kipi-core/commands/design-chain.md, Step 2.) Held by verify.py "study": WARN under 12 cited techniques.
28. Ground the build in the study, not in memory. Order: study, then techniques list, then build, then compare. The first fix for a generic result is to open the reference, not to add effects. (design-chain-gate.py craft_problems, the GROUNDING comment: "the manifest made the build prove it used what it declared, and nothing made the declaration come from anywhere but the builder's own prior".) Held by verify.py "craft": a technique that cites a time STUDY.md does not contain fails.
29. A craft manifest (craft.json) lists the techniques taken, each with the time it cites and a fingerprint, a regex that must be found in index.html. A technique declared and not built fails; a manifest that declares nothing fails ("declaring nothing must not be the cheapest way to comply"). (design-chain-gate.py craft_problems.)
30. Measure the DISTANCE to the reference, not the absence of defects. A page or video with nothing wrong can still be bland, and every defect detector passes it. Floors come from the reference's own measurements, never from taste, and reaching them is a floor and not a finish line: compare.py prints no PASS. (q-system/.q-system/scripts/design-gap-check.py docstring; design-chain-gate.py craft_problems docstring: "when you have a floor, you are programmed to only pass the floor".)
31. Run a control through every instrument. compare.py measures a still clip first and stops if it does not read 0 percent moving, because a probe can be blind or broken and report "fine" about everything. (q-system/.q-system/scripts/design-exemplar-capture.py, the shadow-root comment: "the probe was not describing the site, it was describing its own blindness".)
32. Re-read the whole set when a reference is added. Every reference named in reference.json "references" must appear in STUDY.md, because a new one changes what the others mean. (design-chain-gate.py craft_problems, the roster check: "the group gets re-read, not appended to".)
33. Three directions before building, with the reference techniques each one takes cited, and one pick recorded. A round that builds an already approved pick says `Implements:` instead. Held by verify.py "directions": WARN under three. (plugins/kipi-core/commands/design-chain.md, Step 3; design-chain-gate.py chain_problems.)
34. The vision comes before the brief: the person's own words about what the video is trying to be, quoted under "## Vision" in BRIEF.md. A brief assembled only from constraints is aimed at nothing. Held by verify.py "vision": WARN with no quoted line. (design-chain-gate.py vision_problems.)
35. Critique with dispositions: every weakness found in the stills is written WEAK[tag] in CRITIQUE.md and answered by a DISPOSITION[tag]: line (fixed, or accepted and why). Held by verify.py "critique": a weakness with no disposition fails. (plugins/kipi-core/commands/design-chain.md, Step 6; design-chain-gate.py disposition_problems.)
36. Nothing is shown before the check has run on exactly that file. verify.py writes a receipt bound to the video's sha256; `verify.py <video> --check-receipt` fails if there is none, if the video changed since, or if it holds a FAIL. Stills of one beat (ruling 19, 20) are the exception, since they are how the look is agreed. (plugins/kipi-core/commands/design-chain.md, Step 10; design-chain-gate.py receipt checks.)
37. Readers are independent and counted against the configured number, not against the rows on disk. When rulings.json sets "readers_required", each reader is a fresh session that saw only the finished video or its contact sheet, not the brief, and its row (reader, verdict STAY or LEAVE, why, video_sha256) goes in readers.jsonl. Any LEAVE fails, rows for an older render do not count, and deleting a row lowers the count. (design-chain-gate.py reader_problems.)
38. A new check starts as a warning where its file does not exist yet, and becomes a failure once the file exists. A gate that is red on every older project gets switched off, and a switched-off gate protects nothing. (design-chain-gate.py chain_problems docstring, the grandfathering scar.)
